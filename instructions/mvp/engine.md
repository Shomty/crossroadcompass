# Product Feature Plan: The Synthesis Engine

## 1. The Core Value Proposition (User Perspective)
The user is at a major life crossroads and feels paralyzed. They do not want to learn astrology. They want a definitive answer to two questions: 
1. "Is my desire for change just a temporary feeling, or a real shift?" (Western)
2. "Is right now the actual time to act, or should I wait?" (Vedic)

## 2. Key User Features (The "AHA" Moments)

### A. The "Crossroads" Dashboard
* **The Opportunity Score:** A clear 1 to 10 gauge showing how aligned the user's internal drive is with their external timing.
* **The Unified Verdict:** A single, auto-generated paragraph synthesizing both systems. 
* **Example Output:** "Your internal frustration is peaking due to a Western Uranus transit, but your Vedic Dasha indicates stability is required for three more months. Do not quit your job yet. Prepare your business plan now and launch in October."

### B. The Action Timeline (Next 12 Months)
* A visual roadmap highlighting "Green Light" windows.
* Instead of showing astrological jargon, the timeline marks specific life themes. 
* **Visual Flags:** Marks exact periods where Western transits perfectly overlap with a supportive Vedic sub-period (Antar Dasha). 

### C. Fluid Profile Adjustments
* **Instant Rectification:** Users often doubt their exact birth minute. They can toggle their birth time forward or backward by a few minutes and instantly watch how their "Opportunity Score" and "Action Timeline" shift in real-time. 

## 3. Premium Modules (Monetization Drivers)
To build a sustainable SaaS with a high Monthly Recurring Revenue, the system divides the Synthesis into highly specific, high-stakes life areas.

### The Career & Income Pivot (Module 1)
* **Target:** Users dissatisfied with their current roles or probation periods, looking to transition into entrepreneurship or consulting.
* **Feature:** Calculates the optimal launch window for a side business. It identifies periods supporting high-income generation versus periods requiring skill accumulation. 

### The Relocation & Investment Guide (Module 2)
* **Target:** Users debating moving abroad or making massive financial commitments.
* **Feature:** Analyzes the timing for physical uprooting and property acquisition. 
* **Example Scenario:** Helping a user decide if the current year supports buying foreign real estate on the Mediterranean coast or if the planetary cycles warn against sudden property investments.

### The Family & Relationship Matrix (Module 3)
* **Target:** Users managing family planning or navigating marriage dynamics.
* **Feature:** Maps out periods of domestic harmony versus periods of necessary restructuring, giving parents and partners a heads-up on energetic shifts in the household.

## 4. Delivery Format
The entire product is delivered as a "Decision Support System". The astrological math (Tropical, Sidereal, Lahiri Ayanamsa) is completely hidden. The user only ever interacts with the translation of that math into plain, actionable guidance.
